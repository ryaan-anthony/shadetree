Questions, answered

What counts as a quote?

A quote counts the first time you send it, by email or by copying its link. Drafts, revisions and resends don’t count again. Jobs and invoices never count.

What happens when I reach my limit?

We’ll let you know when you’re close. At the limit you can keep drafting, and you can upgrade in a couple of clicks to send more. Quotes you’ve already sent keep working, and clients can still approve and pay.

Do you charge per user?

No. Each plan includes a number of team members, and technicians who only see their own visits don’t cost extra seats.

Are there fees on card payments?

Card payments go straight to your own account with our payment processor, at their standard rates. You’ll see the exact rate before you connect.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from your settings and you’ll move to Free at the end of your billing period. Nothing is deleted.

Can I take my data with me?

Any time, on any plan. Export your clients, quotes, jobs, invoices and payments as spreadsheets, or download everything, including PDFs and photos, in one file.
