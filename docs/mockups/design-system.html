Shadetree

Design system

Tokens and components behind every mockup. Calm, dense, trustworthy surfaces for staff; the business’s own brand for clients. Boldness is spent in one place: the progress rail that follows work from request to paid.

Principles

  • One action color. Canopy green is for primary actions and progress. Status colors only mean status.
  • Hairlines, not shadows. Panels are separated by 1px borders; shadows are reserved for things that float (menus, dialogs).
  • Lists over cards. Related items live together in one panel, divided by rules, so owners can scan.
  • Money is tabular. Amounts use tabular figures and right alignment so columns line up.
  • Radius has hierarchy. 6px controls, 10px panels, 14px dialogs and client cards.
  • Copy is plain. Sentence case, verbs on buttons, the button’s word repeats in the confirmation.

Brand

Owner decision, 2026-11-21. In the app: BrandHelper and /styleguide.

The name is Shadetree: one word, lower-case t, in copy, titles and emails. The wordmark is “Shade” in green #059669 and “tree” in ink on light backgrounds, or white on dark ones (the marketing footer, the app sidebar). Client pages and emails carry the business’s brand, with Shadetree only in “Powered by”.
