Product mockups

Clickable HTML mockups for milestones M1–M5, built directly from the specs in docs/specs. They show layout, content, states and flows. They are not production code: implementation choices belong to each spec’s developer (see PLT-01 and PLT-05).

How to read these
  • The sample business is Oakline Lawn & Landscape. Its brand color (rust) shows on client pages; Shadetree green is used everywhere staff work.
  • Buttons, menus, dialogs, ⌘K search and the quote builder totals work.
  • Resize to 360 px wide to see the mobile layouts.
