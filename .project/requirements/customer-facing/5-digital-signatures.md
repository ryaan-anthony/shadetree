# Epic: Digital Signature Collection

## Overview
Implement a legally compliant digital signature system that allows customers to sign quotes, contracts, and service agreements electronically.

## Goals
- Create a frictionless signature capture experience
- Ensure legal compliance with e-signature regulations
- Support multiple signature methods (draw, type, click)
- Generate and securely store signed documents
- Enable signature verification and audit trail

## Technical Considerations
- Compliance with e-signature laws (ESIGN Act, UETA)
- Secure document storage with encryption
- Audit trail for signature events
- Mobile-responsive design for touch devices
- Document integrity verification

## User Stories
- As a customer, I want to sign documents electronically so I don't need to print and mail papers
- As a customer, I want to sign on my mobile device so I can complete the process anywhere
- As a customer, I want to choose how I provide my signature so I can use what's most comfortable
- As a business owner, I want legally binding signatures so I have enforceable agreements
- As a business owner, I want a record of when and how documents were signed for legal protection

## Success Metrics
- Digital signature completion rate >90%
- Average signature completion time <1 minute
- Mobile signature rate comparable to desktop
- Document storage success rate 100%
- Legal compliance verification passing rate 100%
