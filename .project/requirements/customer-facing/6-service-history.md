# Epic: Customer Service History

## Overview
Develop a comprehensive service history tracking system that allows customers to view their past services, quotes, invoices, and related documents in one place.

## Goals
- Create a unified view of customer service history
- Enable easy access to past quotes and invoices
- Facilitate repeat business through service history insights
- Support document storage and retrieval for completed services
- Implement feedback collection for service quality improvement

## Features
1. [Service History Dashboard](../customer-facing/service-history/1-history-dashboard.md)
2. [Document Archive and Retrieval](../customer-facing/service-history/2-document-archive.md)
3. [Past Quote and Invoice Access](../customer-facing/service-history/3-past-quotes-invoices.md)
4. [Feedback Collection System](../customer-facing/service-history/4-feedback-collection.md)
5. [Service Timeline Visualization](../customer-facing/service-history/5-service-timeline.md)

## Technical Considerations
- Efficient data retrieval for potentially large history records
- Document storage optimization
- Access control and privacy considerations
- Integration with feedback and review systems
- Mobile-responsive design for all devices

## User Stories
- As a customer, I want to see my service history so I can reference past work
- As a customer, I want access to past invoices for my records or tax purposes
- As a customer, I want to provide feedback on completed services to help improve quality
- As a customer, I want to easily request repeat services based on my history
- As a business owner, I want to collect customer feedback to improve my service quality

## Success Metrics
- Service history access rate >30% of customers
- Document retrieval success rate >95%
- Repeat service request rate from history view >15%
- Feedback submission rate >25% after service completion
- Customer satisfaction with history access >4/5
