# Feature: Lead Generation

## Overview
Contact forms, newsletter signup, and lead capture mechanisms strategically placed throughout the marketing website to convert visitors into qualified leads. This feature focuses on capturing prospect information and routing inquiries to the appropriate sales channels.

## User Stories
- As a potential customer, I want to easily contact the sales team so that I can get personalized assistance with my questions
- As a visitor, I want to subscribe to updates so that I can stay informed about new features and industry insights
- As a business owner, I want to download resources so that I can learn more about best practices for my industry

## Tasks

### 1. Contact Forms
Multi-step contact forms with lead qualification questions and intelligent routing.

**Implementation Notes:**
- Create contact form controller with lead scoring and qualification logic
- Build progressive contact forms for general inquiries, demos, and support requests
- Add form validation with real-time feedback and spam protection using reCAPTCHA
- Implement lead routing system to appropriate sales representatives based on criteria
- Create form analytics tracking with conversion rate monitoring and A/B testing

**Estimation:** 7-9 hours

### 2. Newsletter Subscription
Email capture with content preferences and segmentation options for targeted marketing.

**Implementation Notes:**
- Build newsletter subscription model with preference management and segmentation
- Create subscription forms for blog sidebar, footer, and popup with GDPR compliance
- Add content preference checkboxes for features, industry news, and case studies
- Implement email marketing platform integration with automated welcome sequences
- Create subscription analytics dashboard with growth metrics and engagement tracking

**Estimation:** 6-8 hours

### 3. Resource Downloads
Gated content system for whitepapers, guides, and case studies with lead capture.

**Implementation Notes:**
- Create resource library with gated download system and lead capture forms
- Build resource landing pages with compelling descriptions and social proof
- Add download tracking with lead scoring based on content engagement levels
- Implement progressive profiling to collect additional information over time
- Create automated follow-up email sequences based on downloaded content type

**Estimation:** 8-10 hours
