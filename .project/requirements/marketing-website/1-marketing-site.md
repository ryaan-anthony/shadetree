# Epic: Marketing Site & Content

## Overview
The core marketing website that showcases Shadetree's platform features, communicates value propositions, and converts visitors into leads through compelling content and strategic calls-to-action. This epic covers the public-facing website architecture, content management, and lead generation mechanisms.

## User Stories
- As a potential customer, I want to understand what Shadetree offers so that I can determine if it meets my business needs
- As a business owner, I want to see pricing and features clearly so that I can make an informed purchasing decision
- As a visitor, I want to easily contact the sales team so that I can get personalized assistance

## Features
1. [**Homepage & Landing Pages**](./marketing/01-homepage.md) - Main landing page with hero sections, feature highlights, and conversion elements
2. [**Feature Showcase**](./marketing/02-feature-showcase.md) - Detailed product feature pages with demonstrations and use cases
3. [**Lead Generation**](./marketing/03-lead-generation.md) - Contact forms, newsletter signup, and lead capture mechanisms
