# Epic: Pricing & Subscription Management

## Overview
Transparent pricing displays and subscription management interfaces that enable visitors to understand plan options, select appropriate tiers, and manage their billing preferences. This epic covers pricing page presentation, plan comparison tools, and basic subscription workflow integration.

## User Stories
- As a potential customer, I want to see clear pricing options so that I can choose the right plan for my business
- As a user, I want to compare different plans easily so that I can understand which features are included
- As a customer, I want to upgrade or downgrade my plan so that I can adjust my subscription as my needs change

## Features
1. [**Pricing Display**](./pricing/01-pricing-display.md) - Public pricing pages with plan tiers, features, and cost breakdowns
2. [**Plan Comparison**](./pricing/02-plan-comparison.md) - Interactive comparison tools and feature matrices
3. [**Subscription Selection**](./pricing/03-subscription-selection.md) - Plan selection workflow and signup integration
4. [**Billing Integration**](./pricing/04-billing-integration.md) - Payment processing and subscription management interfaces
