# Initiative: SaaS Owner Backend

## Overview
Develop a lightweight administrative interface for the SaaS platform owners to manage business customers, monitor usage metrics, control feature access, and handle subscription management across the multi-tenant environment.

## Business Objectives
- Create a centralized system for managing business customers
- Implement feature toggles and subscription management capabilities
- Provide usage analytics for business intelligence
- Support billing management and feature flag control
- Enable secure support tools for customer assistance

## Epics
1. [Customer Management](saas-owner/1-customer-management.md) - View, search, and manage business customers with support tools
2. [Feature Toggle System](saas-owner/2-feature-toggles.md) - Control feature availability per business customer or globally
3. [Subscription Management](saas-owner/3-subscription-management.md) - Create plans, assign subscriptions, track usage, and handle plan changes
4. [Usage Analytics](saas-owner/4-usage-analytics.md) - View usage metrics and generate basic reports
5. [Billing Management](saas-owner/5-billing-management.md) - Handle invoicing, payments, billing cycles, and financial reporting
6. [Support and Impersonation](saas-owner/6-support-impersonation.md) - Secure tools for support staff to assist customers
