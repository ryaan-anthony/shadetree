# Epic: Subscription Management

## Overview
Develop a comprehensive subscription management system that enables platform administrators to create subscription plans, manage customer subscriptions, track usage against limits, and handle upgrades/downgrades.

## User Stories
- As a platform administrator, I want to create and manage subscription plans so I can offer different service tiers
- As a customer success manager, I want to assign subscriptions to customers so I can control their access levels
- As a billing administrator, I want to track usage against limits so I can enforce subscription boundaries
- As a sales representative, I want to upgrade/downgrade customer plans so I can meet changing customer needs
- As a marketing manager, I want to manage trial subscriptions so I can convert prospects to paid customers

## Features
1. [Plan Creation and Management](subscription-management/1-plan-management.md) - Tools for defining subscription tiers, pricing, features, and limits for different customer segments
2. [Customer Subscription Assignment](subscription-management/2-subscription-assignment.md) - System for assigning, modifying, and managing customer subscription plans and statuses
3. [Usage Tracking and Limits](subscription-management/3-usage-tracking.md) - Real-time monitoring of customer usage against subscription limits with enforcement capabilities
4. [Upgrade/Downgrade Workflows](subscription-management/4-plan-changes.md) - Automated processes for handling subscription changes with proration and billing adjustments
5. [Trial Management System](subscription-management/5-trial-management.md) - Complete trial lifecycle management from signup to conversion or expiration
