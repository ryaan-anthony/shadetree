# Epic: Service & Pricing Management

## Overview
Service & Pricing Management enables businesses to define, configure, and manage their service offerings, pricing structures, packages, and promotional strategies. This includes service categorization, dynamic pricing, seasonal adjustments, and package deals.

## User Stories
- As a business owner, I want to create and manage my services so customers know what I offer
- As a business owner, I want to run promotions and discounts so I can attract new customers

## Features

1. [**Service Catalog Management**](./service-pricing/01-service-catalog.md) - Create, organize, and manage service offerings with descriptions, requirements, and categorization
2. [**Pricing Structure Configuration**](./service-pricing/02-pricing-structure.md) - Set base prices, pricing tiers, and complex pricing rules based on various factors
3. [**Service Packages & Bundles**](./service-pricing/03-packages-bundles.md) - Create combination packages and bundled services for increased value offerings
4. [**Promotions & Discounts**](./service-pricing/04-promotions-discounts.md) - Manage promotional campaigns, discount codes, and special pricing events
5. [**Add-ons & Upsells**](./service-pricing/05-addons-upsells.md) - Configure service add-ons, cross-sells, and upsell suggestions
6. [**Seasonal & Dynamic Pricing**](./service-pricing/06-dynamic-pricing.md) - Implement time-based pricing, demand-based adjustments, and seasonal rates
7. [**Price Change Audit Trail**](./service-pricing/07-price-audit-trail.md) - Maintain a history of price changes for compliance and transparency
8. [**Quote & Invoice Customization**](./service-pricing/08-quote-invoice.md) - Customize quotes and invoices with service details and terms
