# Epic: Staff Management

## Overview
Staff Management provides streamlined tools for managing team members, their roles, permissions, and schedules within the service business. This includes onboarding, role assignment, and availability tracking.

## User Stories
- As a business owner, I want to add new staff members so I can grow my team
- As a manager, I want to assign roles and permissions so staff can access appropriate functions
- As a staff member, I want to manage my availability so customers can book with me

## Features

1. [**Staff Onboarding & Profile Management**](./staff/01-staff-profiles.md) - Add new team members, manage their profiles, contact information, and basic settings
2. [**Role & Permission Management**](./staff/02-roles-permissions.md) - Define roles, assign permissions, and manage access control for different staff levels
3. [**Staff Scheduling & Availability**](./staff/03-scheduling-availability.md) - Manage work schedules and availability windows
4. [**Service Assignments**](./staff/04-service-assignments.md) - Assign staff members to specific services and manage their service capabilities
