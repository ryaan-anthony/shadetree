# Epic: Notifications

## Overview
Notifications provides a comprehensive communication system for automated messaging, alerts, and notifications across multiple channels including email, SMS, in-app notifications, and push notifications. This ensures timely communication with customers and staff.

## User Stories
- As a business owner, I want to send automated booking confirmations so customers feel confident
- As a customer, I want to receive reminders so I don't miss my appointment
- As a staff member, I want notifications about schedule changes so I stay informed
- As a business owner, I want to customize message templates so they match my brand

## Features

1. [**Message Template Management**](./notifications/01-message-templates.md) - Create, edit, and manage email and SMS templates for various business scenarios
2. [**Automated Notification Triggers**](./notifications/02-automated-triggers.md) - Configure automatic notifications based on booking events, schedule changes, and business rules
3. [**Multi-Channel Delivery**](./notifications/03-multi-channel-delivery.md) - Send notifications via email, SMS, push notifications, and in-app alerts
4. [**Customer Communication Preferences**](./notifications/04-customer-preferences.md) - Allow customers to manage their notification preferences and communication channels
5. [**Broadcast Messaging**](./notifications/05-broadcast-messaging.md) - Send bulk messages to customer segments or all customers for announcements
6. [**Delivery Tracking & Analytics**](./notifications/06-delivery-analytics.md) - Track notification delivery, open rates, and engagement metrics
7. [**Notification History & Logs**](./notifications/07-history-logs.md) - Maintain a history of sent notifications for auditing and customer support
