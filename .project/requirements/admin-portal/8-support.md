# Epic: Support

## Overview
The Support system enables business owners to manage customer inquiries, support requests, and internal help resources through an integrated ticketing system.

## User Stories
- As a business owner, I want to receive and respond to customer inquiries in one place
- As a business owner, I want to track support ticket status and resolution times
- As a business owner, I want to escalate complex issues to management or platform support
- As a customer, I want to submit support requests and track their progress
- As a staff member, I want to collaborate on resolving customer issues

## Features

1. [**Customer Support Ticketing**](./support/customer-support-ticketing.md) - Complete ticketing system for managing customer inquiries and support requests
2. [**Internal Communication & Collaboration**](./support/internal-communication-collaboration.md) - Tools for staff collaboration on customer support issues
3. [**Knowledge Base & Help Resources**](./support/knowledge-base-help-resources.md) - Self-service resources and internal documentation system
4. [**Customer Communication Hub**](./support/customer-communication-hub.md) - Unified communication interface for all customer touchpoints
5. [**Platform Support Integration**](./support/platform-support-integration.md) - Direct access to ShadeTree platform support and escalation
