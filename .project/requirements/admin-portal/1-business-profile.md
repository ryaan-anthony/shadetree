# Epic: Business Profile Management

## Overview
Business Profile Management enables business owners to configure and maintain their company information, operating hours, location details, and core business settings that affect how their service business operates and appears to customers.

## User Stories
- As a business owner, I want to update my business information so customers see accurate details
- As a business owner, I want to set my operating hours so customers know when I'm available
- As a business owner, I want to manage my service locations so customers can find me

## Features
1. [**Basic Business Information**](./business-profile/01-basic-information.md) - Core business details including name, description, contact information, and branding
2. [**Operating Hours Management**](./business-profile/02-operating-hours.md) - Flexible scheduling system for regular hours, holidays, and special availability
3. [**Location & Service Areas**](./business-profile/03-locations.md) - Physical location management and service delivery area configuration
4. [**Business Branding**](./business-profile/04-branding.md) - Logo, colors, and visual identity management for customer-facing interfaces
