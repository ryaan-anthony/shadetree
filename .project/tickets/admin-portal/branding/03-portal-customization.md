# Feature: Portal Customization

## Overview
Create a customization system that allows businesses to brand their customer and staff portals with their unique look and feel.

## User Stories

1. Portal Theming
   - As a business owner
   - I want to customize portal appearance
   - So it matches our brand identity

2. Layout Configuration
   - As an administrator
   - I want to configure portal layouts
   - So information is organized our way

3. Custom Domain
   - As a business owner
   - I want to use our own domain
   - So customers see our brand URL

4. Widget Customization
   - As a portal manager
   - I want to customize portal widgets
   - So we show relevant information

5. Mobile Adaptation
   - As a business owner
   - I want the portal to look good on mobile
   - So customers can access it anywhere
