Our Story

How we're revolutionizing service business management

2020

The Beginning

After years of running service businesses and struggling with fragmented software solutions, our founders decided to build the platform they wished they had. Starting in a garage with a simple scheduling tool.

2022

Rapid Growth

Expanded our platform to include customer management, invoicing, and payment processing. Reached 1,000 active businesses and secured Series A funding to accelerate development.

2024

AI Integration

Launched AI-powered route optimization and predictive analytics. Crossed 10,000 businesses and expanded internationally, helping service companies across 15 countries.

2025

The Future

Continuing to innovate with advanced automation, industry-specific solutions, and deeper integrations. Our goal: to become the operating system for every service business.
