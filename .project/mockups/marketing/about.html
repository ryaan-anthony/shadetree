About ShadeTree

We're on a mission to simplify service business operations, helping entrepreneurs and small businesses focus on what they do best while we handle the rest.

Our Mission

Service businesses are the backbone of every community, yet they often struggle with outdated booking systems, manual scheduling, and complex customer management processes.

ShadeTree was born from the belief that powerful business management tools shouldn't be complicated or expensive. We've created a platform that's intuitive enough for anyone to use, yet robust enough to scale with growing businesses.

Our goal is simple: empower service professionals to spend more time serving customers and less time managing administrative tasks.

Our Story

ShadeTree started when our founders, both former service business owners, experienced firsthand the frustration of juggling multiple tools just to manage basic business operations. Between scheduling apps, payment processors, customer databases, and communication tools, running a service business felt more like managing software than serving customers.

After selling their businesses, they decided to build the solution they wished they'd had: an all-in-one platform designed specifically for service businesses. They partnered with experienced software engineers and UX designers to create ShadeTree.

Today, ShadeTree serves thousands of service professionals across various industries, from home cleaning services to professional consultants. We're proud to be a part of their success stories.
