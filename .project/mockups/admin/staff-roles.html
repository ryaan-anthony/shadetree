Security Guidelines

Principle of Least Privilege

Grant users only the minimum permissions needed to perform their job functions.

Regular Permission Review

Review and update staff permissions quarterly or when roles change.

Sensitive Operations

Payment processing and data export require admin-level permissions.

Role Assignment

All staff members must be assigned a role before accessing the system.

Role Descriptions

Owner: Full system access, business management
Admin: Most operations except ownership transfers
Manager: Quote management, scheduling, customer service
Technician: View own schedule, update job status
