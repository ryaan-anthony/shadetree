## **🔑 Sales Incentive Strategies**

### **1\. Referral Program (Customer-Led)**

Turn your customers into salespeople.

* **Incentive:**  
   Give customers a month free, cash bonus, or $XX credit for every referral.  
* **Tooling:**  
   Use tools like [Rewardful](https://www.rewardful.com), [FirstPromoter](https://firstpromoter.com), or even manual coupon codes.  
* **Tactic:**  
   Add a “Refer & Earn” tab inside the dashboard — dead simple.

**Why it works:** Service businesses talk. If one likes your product, they’ll tell their peers.

---

### **2\. Affiliate Program (Professional-Led)**

Target web designers, IT consultants, and agency folks who set up tools for SMBs.

* **Incentive:**  
   20–30% recurring commissions (common in SaaS)  
* **Approach:**  
   Build a small network of 5–10 early affiliates, give them landing pages or onboarding packs  
* **Bonus tactic:**  
   Offer co-branded white-label versions (e.g. “QuoteTool powered by YourBrand”)

**Why it works:** These pros often act as “advisors” to your target market.

---

### **3\. Freemium Model or $1 Trial**

Eliminate friction for tech-skeptical users.

* **Option A:** Basic free tier with upgrade prompts (limit quotes/calendar sync/etc.)  
* **Option B:** $1 for 7 days of full access, then convert  
* **Option C:** “Free for first job” — they only pay after they win one job using your system.

**Why it works:** Your audience doesn’t want to gamble money to *try something*. This flips the risk.

 Offer IT support companies, web dev shops, or field service consultants a way to bundle your tool with their services.

## **📅 Standard Setup: "4-Year Vesting with 1-Year Cliff"**

This means:

| Timeline | Equity Earned |
| ----- | ----- |
| 0–12 months | **0%** |
| After 12 months | **25%** |
| Months 13–48 | **1/48th per month** (2.08%) |

### **🔹 “1-Year Cliff”**

* No equity is earned until the **first full year** is complete.  
* If someone leaves before 12 months: **they get nothing**.  
* It’s a safety net to prevent giving equity to short-timers.

### **🔹 “Monthly Vesting After the Cliff”**

* After the first year, they earn equity **monthly** (or quarterly).  
* By year 4, they are **fully vested** and own 100% of their granted shares.

---

## **🧮 Example: 30% Equity with Standard Vesting**

Let’s say you grant your co-founder 30% equity on a 4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff:

| Time Elapsed | Total Vested |
| ----- | ----- |
| 0–11 months | 0% |
| 12 months | 7.5% (25% of 30%) |
| 18 months | \~11.25% |
| 24 months | 15% |
| 48 months | 30% (fully vested) |

If they leave after 10 months → they get **0%**.  
If they leave after 2 years → they keep **15%**.

---

## **🔐 Why It’s Critical**

* **Protects you** from a co-founder ghosting or under-delivering  
* **Protects them** if you change your mind after 11 months  
* Aligns incentives: everyone earns ownership by contributing over time  
* Gives you flexibility in case you need to part ways

---

## **📄 Implementation Tips**

* Use **Founder Stock Purchase Agreements** or SAFE docs with vesting language  
* If forming an LLC, use **member units \+ vesting schedule** in the operating agreement  
* Include **IP assignment** to ensure all work belongs to the company

## **🧩 1\. Define the Sales Motion**

If your family member is helping with **cold calling**, **demo setting**, or **closing**, know which part of the funnel they own:

| Sales Role | Task | Common Commission Style |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Lead Generator | Call & qualify prospects (not close) | Flat rate per qualified lead ($5–$25) |
| Appointment Setter | Schedule demos for you | $10–$50 per kept appointment |
| Closer | Handles demo \+ closes sale | % of MRR or flat per sale |
| Full-Cycle Sales | Does everything | Highest % of MRR (10–30% possible) |

---

## **💵 2\. Commission-Only Compensation Ideas**

### **Option A: % of First Payment (Simple & Cash-Flow Safe)**

* Example: **30–50% of first month’s payment**  
* $49/month plan → $15–$25 commission

✅ Easy to track  
✅ No long-term commitment  
❌ Not as exciting if you want them motivated long-term

---

### **Option B: % of Monthly Revenue (Recurring Commission)**

* Example: **10–20% of monthly payment** for as long as the customer stays  
* $49/month → $5–$10/month recurring

✅ Great for recurring incentive  
✅ Keeps them invested in reducing churn  
❌ Can add up — you may want to **cap it** at 12 months or $X

---

### **Option C: Tiered Rewards (Performance-Based)**

* First 5 sales: 20%  
* Next 10: 25%  
* 20+ per month: 30% or bonus ($200+)

✅ Gamifies performance  
✅ Keeps them hustling

---

### **Option D: Flat Rate \+ Bonus**

* $50 per sale \+ $250 bonus for every 10 sales/month  
* Or: $25/lead, $50/sale if they do both

---

## **🛡️ 3\. Protect Yourself with Clear Terms**

Even if it’s family:

* **Write a simple agreement**:  
  * Commission structure  
  * Payment timing (monthly? quarterly?)

  * What happens if a customer churns quickly  
* Consider a **"minimum retention" clause**:

   *“Commission is paid after 30 days of active subscription”*  
   Prevents payouts for cancellations or refunds.
