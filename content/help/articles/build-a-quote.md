A quote is saved as you build it and stays a draft until you send it. Your client sees nothing
until then, so take your time.

## Start a quote

1. Open **Quotes** and press **New quote**. (From a request, press **Create quote** instead:
   see [Take a request and turn it into a quote](take-requests).)
2. Under **Client**, search by name, phone, email or address, and choose them.
3. Press **Start quote**.

## Add what you're doing

![The quote builder: choose the client, add items with Add a service, check the totals, then press Send quote](quote-builder)

1. Give it a **Title** if you like, such as "Spring cleanup". It's shown at the top of the quote.
2. Check **Valid until**. After that date your client can't approve it without asking you. The
   starting length comes from your settings.
3. Under **Items**, use **Add a service** to pick from your
   [services and prices](add-services-and-prices). The name, description and price come across,
   and you can change any of them on this quote.
4. For something you don't keep as a service, press **Custom line** and type it in
   **Item or service**.
5. Set **Qty**, **Unit** and **Price** for each line. Use **Discount** on a line for money off
   that line, as a percent or an amount.
6. Untick **Taxed** on any line that shouldn't have tax.

Use the arrows beside a line to move it up or down, and the bin to remove it.

## Discounts and tax on the whole quote

Under **Totals**:

- **Quote discount** takes a percent or an amount off the whole quote.
- **Tax** lists your tax rates. Tick the ones that apply to this quote. Your client's tax
  exemption and your default rates are already applied.

## Add a message, terms and photos

- **Message to client** appears at the top of the quote, like "Thanks for having us out."
- **Terms** start from your settings. Change them here for this quote only.
- **Internal notes** are for your team. They're never shown to the client.
- **Attachments** adds photos or PDFs your client can see with the quote.

## Check it, then send it

- The word **Saved** at the top tells you your changes are kept.
- **Preview as client** opens the quote exactly as your client will see it, in a new tab.
- The **···** menu has **Download PDF**, and **Delete draft** if you don't need it.

When it's ready, press **Send quote**. See
[Send a quote, and what your client sees](send-a-quote).
