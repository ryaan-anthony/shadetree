A job is the work you've agreed to do. It holds what needs doing, its visits on the schedule, and
the invoices for it.

## When your client approves

When your client presses **Approve quote**, you're told, and the quote shows **Approved**, with who
approved it and when. If **Make a job when a quote is approved** is on in **Settings**, under
**Quotes, jobs & invoices**, the job is made for you, and it takes all the work on the quote. If it's off, open the quote
and press **Create job**.

## They said yes by phone?

Open the quote and press **Mark approved**. Type **Who approved** and **How they approved**, like
"Approved on the phone on Tuesday", and press **Mark approved**. It's recorded with your name and
your note, and the quote shows **Approved**. If **Make a job when a quote is approved** is on, the
job is made too.

## Split a quote's work among jobs

Press **Create job** on a quote and you choose what this job will do. Every service still to do
is ticked, with how much of it is left, like "4 of 10 to do".

![Create a job: untick what this job won't do, or lower a quantity](job-picker)

1. Untick a service this job won't do.
2. To do only part of a service, lower its number. Six of ten trees this week, say.
3. Press **Create job**.

What you leave out stays to do. Press **Create job** again for the next job. If you ask for more
than is left, you're told which job already has it. The quote lists its jobs, and each job says
which quote it came from.

## Work through the job

![A job: schedule visits, see what your crew logged, press Complete when it's done](job-page)

- **Schedule visit** books the work on the schedule. See
  [Book visits on the schedule](book-visits-on-the-schedule).
- **Add instructions** for the crew: what to bring, where to start. **Access notes** (gate codes and
  the like) come from the client's property.
- Your crew adds photos and notes on each visit from **My day**, and logs what they did under
  **The work**. Their totals show beside each item, and in **Work log**.
- **Add to-do work** takes more of the quote's work into the job.
- When the work is done, press **Complete**. It asks how much of each item was done, starting from
  what the crew logged. Completing is final: work not done stays off this job. If your crew added
  something on site, give it a price here.
- Then **Create invoice** bills what was done. See
  [Invoice finished work and record a payment](invoice-finished-work).

## Plans change

- **Cancel job** stops a job the client no longer wants, with a reason. Its open visits are
  cancelled too. You can **Reopen** it later.
- **Reopen** a completed job if you need to go back.
