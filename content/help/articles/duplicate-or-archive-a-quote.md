## Copy a quote

Doing similar work again? Open the quote, then in the **···** menu choose:

- **Duplicate**: a new draft for the same client, with the same lines;
- **Duplicate for another client**: choose the client, then press the button to make the copy.

The copy opens as a draft. Prices come across as they were on the original, so check them.

## Archive a quote

Quotes you're done with (declined, expired, or just old) can be archived to clear them from your
lists:

1. Open the quote.
2. In the **···** menu, choose **Archive**.

Archived quotes are kept, with everything that happened on them. Find them on **Quotes** under
**Archived**, and bring one back with **Restore from archive** in its **···** menu.

## Delete a draft

A draft that was never sent can be deleted instead: open it, and in the **···** menu choose
**Delete draft**. That can't be undone. Sent quotes can't be deleted, only archived.
