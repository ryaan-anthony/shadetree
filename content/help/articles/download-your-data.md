Your data is yours: download any of it, any time, on any plan. Each file opens in any spreadsheet
app.

![Your data: press Download CSV beside a list, or Export everything for one zip with all of it](your-data)

## Download one list

1. Open **Settings**, then **Plan and data**, **Your data**.
2. Under **Download as spreadsheets**, press **Download CSV** beside the list you want: clients,
   quotes, jobs, invoices, payments and more.
3. Open the file in your spreadsheet app.

## Download everything

1. Under **Everything in one download**, press **Export everything**.
2. It's put together in the background. You get a notification when it's ready.
3. Come back and press **Download the zip**. To get a fresher copy later, press
   **Make a new one**.

## Reading the files

- Money is a plain number in the document's currency, like 1240.00.
- Dates are written YYYY-MM-DD; times are in your business's time zone.
- Columns stay the same over time, and new ones are added at the end, so a spreadsheet you build on
  them keeps working.
- Spreadsheet apps drop the leading zero from postal codes like 02134. Import that column as text
  to keep it.

## Who can download

Owners, Admins, and anyone whose role includes exporting data. Every export and download is
listed under **Data log**, with who made it and when.
