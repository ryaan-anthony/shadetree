The bell at the top of every page shows what needs you: a new request, a quote approved, a
payment, a visit you're on that's moved. The number on it is how many you haven't read.

![Notifications: open one to go to what it's about, or press Mark all as read](notifications)

## Read them

1. Press the bell.
2. Open a notification to go to what it's about. It's marked read.
3. **Mark as read** marks one without opening it; **Mark all as read** marks them all.

Notifications are kept for 90 days. You only get the ones your role can act on: technicians hear
about their own visits and follow-ups, the office about requests, quotes and payments.

## Choose what comes by email

1. On **Notifications**, press **Email settings**.
2. Under **Email me when**, tick what you also want by email, such as **New requests**,
   **Quote approved** or **Online payments**. Untick the rest.
3. Press **Save**.

![Email settings: tick what you also want by email, then press Save](email-settings)

Everything still shows under the bell. Emails start once you've confirmed your email address.
Your choices are yours alone, and for this business only.
