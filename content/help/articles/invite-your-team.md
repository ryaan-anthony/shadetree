Everyone on your team signs in as themselves. Their role decides what they can see and do.

![Team: type an email and choose a role under Invite someone, then press Send invitation](team-page)

## Invite someone

1. Open **Team**.
2. Under **Invite someone**, type their **Email** and choose their **Role**.
3. Press **Send invitation**.

They get an email with a link to join. Until they do, the invitation is listed under
**Invitations**, where you can **Resend** it, **Copy link** to send it yourself, or **Revoke** it.

## Roles

Owners see a **Roles** button on **Team** (and a **See what each role can do** link) that lists
what each role allows.

![The Technician role on Roles: read what it can do before you give it to someone](roles-page)

- **Owner** runs the business, including billing and the plan. There's always at least one.
- **Admin** runs the office: clients, quotes, the schedule, invoices and the team, and can also
  change Settings and download the business's data.
- **Technician** works their own visits from their phone, and drafts quotes for the requests given
  to them for the office to check and send.

On a paid plan you can make roles of your own, like a dispatcher: press **New role**, name it,
tick what people with it can do, and press **Create role**. Billing and the plan, deleting the
business, the payment account and who's an Owner always stay with Owners.

To change someone's role, choose a new one beside their name on **Team** and press **Save**.

## When someone leaves

Press **Deactivate** beside their name. They lose access straight away, and their name stays on
the work they did. **Reactivate** gives it back.

## How many people

Your plan sets how many people can be on your team, counting you and invitations not yet
accepted. See [Your plan and its limits](your-plan).
