Your **account** is you: your name, email and password, the same in every business you belong
to. Your **profile** is how you appear in one business: a photo, title and bio, which clients may
see on visit messages if the business turns that on.

## Sign in, and if you forget your password

- On the sign-in page, enter your email and password and press **Sign in**. Tick
  **Keep me signed in** on your own device to stay signed in for 4 weeks.
- Forgot it? Press **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and press **Send reset link**. Open the
  link in the email, choose a **New password**, and press **Save password and sign in**. If the
  link has expired, press **Request a new one**.

## Change your name, email or password

1. In the menu, press your name (at the bottom on a computer, or in the menu on a phone). This
   opens **Your account**.
2. Change your first or last name, your email, or type a **New password** and
   **Confirm new password**. Changing your email sends a confirmation link to the new address.
3. Type your current password (it's needed to save any change here) and press **Save changes**.

## Signed in somewhere you shouldn't be?

On **Your account**, **Where you're signed in** lists your browsers. **Sign out** beside one signs
it out; **Sign out others** signs out everywhere except this browser. Changing your password does
that too.

## Your profile in a business

1. In the menu, press **Your profile in** and the business's name.
2. Add a **Photo**: a clear photo of your face helps clients know who's at the door.
3. Add your **Title**, like "Lead technician", a **Short bio**, and a work phone if you have one.
4. Press **Save profile**.

![Your profile: add a photo, a title and a short bio, then press Save profile](your-profile)

People who manage the team (Owners and Admins) can change others' profiles from **Team**, under
**Profile**; only Owners can change an Owner's. The person is told.

Clients see profiles only if the business turns that on:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Messages**.
2. Under **Team profiles**, tick **Show team profiles to clients**.
3. Press **Save team profiles**.

Clients then see the photo, first name, title and work phone of the people going, never a last
name, email or personal number.

## Leaving Shadetree

**Delete account**, at the bottom of **Your account**, removes your name, email and password and
signs you out everywhere. The work you did stays with each business, shown as "Former member".
If you're a business's only Owner, make someone else an Owner first.
