**Schedule** shows every visit for the week or the day, colored by who's going. Your crew sees
the visits they're on in **My day** on their phone.

![The week's schedule: visits on the calendar by day and time, and the Crew list with each person's booked hours](schedule-week)

## Find your way around

- **Today** and the arrows move between weeks (or days). **Day** and **Week** switch the view.
- **Crew** lists your team with the hours booked for each. Choose a person to see only their
  visits, or **Not assigned** for visits nobody has yet.
- **Color by** colors visits by **Person** or by **Status**: **Scheduled**, **In progress** or
  **Completed**.
- Days you're closed are shaded, from **Settings**, **Hours**.

## Book a visit

1. Press **New visit**, or choose an open time on the calendar.
2. Under **Job**, choose the job. Jobs that **Need scheduling** are listed first. Press
   **Continue**.
3. Pick the **Date (or first day)**. For work over several days, open **More than one day**: each day becomes its
   own visit.
4. Under **Arrival**, choose **A start time**, **A window** (for example 1 to 3 pm) or
   **Anytime that day**.
5. Set the **Estimated hours**.
6. Tick **Who's going**.
7. Add **Instructions for this visit** for your crew, and a **Note for the client** if you like.
8. Press **Schedule visit**.

![Schedule a visit: the date, arrival, estimated hours, who's going and the notes](schedule-a-visit)

## Tell the client, or not

If the client has an email address and you send visit emails, a box such as
"Email Maria Lopez about this" is ticked, and they're emailed the date and time. Untick it to book without telling them this time.
Several quick changes arrive as one email a few minutes later.

## Move or change a visit

Drag a visit to a new time or day on the calendar, or open it and change it.

Your crew sees each change on their phone the next time they look at **My day**.
