If a client asks you to forget them, you can erase their details. It can't be undone. Your
quotes, jobs, invoices and payments stay, with their numbers and totals, so your books still
add up.

To stop working with someone without erasing them, use **Archive** instead: see
[Add a client and keep their details](add-and-find-clients).

## Erase their details

1. Open the client.
2. In the **···** menu, choose **Erase their details…**.
3. Read what it removes and what it keeps.
4. Type the client's name to confirm.
5. Press **Erase their details**.

![Erase their details: read what's removed and kept, type the client's name, then press Erase their details](erase-client)

## What's removed

- Their name, company, emails, phones and addresses, and your notes and timeline about them.
- Their details wherever else they appear: titles, line items, payment notes, PDFs, history, and
  your other clients' notes.
- Messages, instructions and internal notes on their documents, jobs and visits, and who signed
  their approvals.
- Photos and files from their requests, visits, quotes, invoices and jobs.
- Any full export you've made, since it may hold their details. Make a new one afterwards if you
  need it.

## What's kept

Their quotes, jobs, invoices and payments, with their numbers, line items and totals, under
"Erased client". Nothing more can be sent to them. The erasure is listed in **Your data**,
**Data log**.
