To answer a question, the Shadetree team may need to see what you see. They can view your
account as one of your people, and every time is recorded on your **Team** page.

![Shadetree support access on Team: check when support viewed your account, as whom and for how long](support-access)

## See when they looked

Open **Team** and scroll to **Shadetree support access**. Each line says whose view they used,
for how long, and when, and whether it was **read-only** or **changes allowed** (with how many
changes). If support hasn't viewed your account, it says so.

## What they can do

- They see the pages that person would see. Usually that's read-only: they can look, not change.
- Some pages they can't open at all while viewing as someone, such as downloads and sign-in.
- Replies to your questions still come through **Help**, **Your questions**. See
  [Get help from the Shadetree team](get-help-from-shadetree).
