With card payments on, every invoice you send has a **Pay** button for whatever's left to pay.
Your client pays from their phone or computer, and the payment shows on the invoice by itself.

## Set it up

Only the business owner can set up card payments.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Money**, **Card payments**.
2. Press the button to set up payments. You go to our card payment provider's secure pages, which
   ask for your business and bank details. It takes a few minutes, and you can stop and come back
   with **Continue setup**.
3. When the provider has checked your details, **Card payments** says you're connected.

Setup makes a new payments account just for this business. Card payments are available for
businesses in the United States.

## How your clients pay

Clients see a **Pay** button on invoices that have something left to pay. If you turn on
**Let clients pay part of an invoice**, they can choose how much to pay now.

When they pay, the invoice is updated straight away, and you're told. You don't record card
payments yourself.

## Your money

- You're the merchant: payouts go to your own bank account on the provider's schedule.
- The provider's card fees apply to each payment.
- Payouts, refunds and disputes are handled in the provider's dashboard, linked from
  **Card payments**. Refunds you make there show on the invoice here.

Cash, checks and transfers still work as before: see
[Invoice finished work and record a payment](invoice-finished-work).
