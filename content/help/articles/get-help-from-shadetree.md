Stuck on something, found something that isn't working, or have an idea? Ask us from the page
you're on. We can see which page you came from and which browser you use, so you don't have to
explain that part.

## Send a question

1. Open **Help** at the top of any page (the question-mark button) and choose **Ask Shadetree for help**.
2. Under **Subject**, say what you're trying to do in a few words, like "Take tax off one invoice".
3. Pick a **Category**: **Question**, **Something isn't working**, **Billing** or **Feature request**.
4. Under **Message**, tell us what you expected and what happened instead.
5. If a picture would help, add up to five **Screenshots**. Leave out your clients' details unless
   they matter to the question.
6. Press **Send**.

You'll see "Thanks. We'll reply here and by email." Your question now has its own page.

> To attach screenshots, your email address needs to be confirmed first. You can still send the
> question without them.

## Follow the answer

We reply on the question's page and by email. To see every question your business has asked,
open **Help** and choose **Your questions**.

Each question shows where it stands:

- **Open**: it's with us.
- **Waiting on you**: we've replied and need something from you. If we don't hear back within a
  week, we mark it resolved and tell you.
- **Resolved**: we think it's sorted. If it isn't, write in **Reply to reopen** and press
  **Send reply**, and it opens again.

## Who can see your questions

- Owners and office staff see every question the business has asked.
- Technicians see only the questions they asked themselves.
- Questions about **Billing** are seen only by the person who asked and by people who can manage
  the plan and billing.

Your clients never see anything you send us.
