Your plan sets how many quotes you can send each billing period, how many people can be on your
team, and which features you have.

![Your plan's limits, with how much of each you've used: check here before you reach a limit](plan-page)

## Check where you are

The menu shows your quotes this billing period, like "6 of 30", and when the count resets. Open
**Settings**, then **Plan and data**, **Plan** for the details and **Compare plans**.

A quote counts when it's first sent. Sending a revision or a copy doesn't count again.

## At the limit

From 80% of your quotes, Shadetree warns you. When you reach the limit:

- New quotes can't be sent until the count resets. The page says when.
- Drafts still save, so you can keep working.
- Quotes you've already sent can still be sent again.

## Change your plan

On **Plan**, press **Choose a plan** under **Compare plans**. Only the business owner can change
the plan and its billing. Moving to a smaller plan turns off features it doesn't include, but
deletes nothing.

## Your data

Your data is yours, on any plan. **Your data**, under **Plan and data**, downloads any of it as
spreadsheets, or everything in one download.
