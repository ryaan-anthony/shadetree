One sign-in can belong to several businesses, for example your own and one you help out at. Each
business keeps its own clients, quotes, team and settings; nothing is shared between them.

## Switch

1. At the top of the menu, press the business's name. It shows your role there underneath.
2. Under **Switch business**, choose the one you want. A tick marks the one you're in.

Shadetree remembers your choice on every device. Your role, notifications and profile are
separate in each business.

## Start another business

In the same menu, press **Create a business**, then fill in its name and details. You're its
Owner. It starts on its own plan, with its own setup steps on **Home**.
