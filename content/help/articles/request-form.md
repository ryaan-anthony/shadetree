Your request form is a page of your own where new clients ask for a quote: what they need, where,
when, and photos. Their request lands in **Requests**, and everyone with office access who keeps
**New requests** ticked in their email settings gets an email.

![Request form settings: tick Take requests online, write an intro, choose the services, then press Save changes](request-form-settings)

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings**, then **Request form**.
2. Tick **Take requests online**. When it's off, the page shows your phone and email instead.
3. Add an **Intro**, shown at the top of the form: say how fast you reply, or what to include.
4. Choose which services clients can pick. Press **Edit services** to change them: each service
   has **Show on your request form**.
5. Change the **Link name** if you like. It's the end of your link.
6. Tick **Let search engines find your request page** if you want people searching for your
   business to land on it.
7. Press **Save changes**.

## Share it

Under **Your request link**, press **Copy link** and put it on your website, social pages and
email signature. **QR code** downloads a code to print on flyers or your truck. **Copy form link**
on **Requests** copies it too. With more than one location, each has a link of its own.

## What clients see

Your form, with your logo and color, titled **Request a quote**. They fill in:

- **What can we help with?**: the services you chose, or **Not sure**;
- **Tell us about the job**: what needs doing, how big it is;
- **When would you like it done?**;
- **Photos**, if they have them;
- **Your name**, an **Email** or **Phone**, and the **Service address**;
- **Which location is nearest?**, if you have more than one.

They press **Send request** and see **Request sent**. The request shows in **Requests** as
**New**, from **Request form**. If their email or phone matches a client you already have, it's
added to that client. See [Take a request and turn it into a quote](take-requests).
