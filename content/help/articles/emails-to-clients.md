Every email to a client comes from your business, with your name and colors. Replies come to you.

![Emails to clients: press View to read an email as your client sees it, and choose your visit emails](emails-to-clients)

## See and change the emails

Open **Settings**, then **Messages**, **Emails to clients**. Every email is listed: quotes, jobs
and invoices, and visits. **View** shows one as your client sees it. On plans that include it,
**Edit** changes its wording.

## Visit emails

Under **Visit emails to clients**, choose which visit emails go out:

- when you book visits (several booked together are one email),
- when you move a visit to another day or time,
- when you cancel a visit or its job,
- **Reminder the day before**, for visits still booked,
- and **On my way**, off until you turn it on: whoever's going taps **On my way**, and the client
  gets their arrival time.

Press **Save visit emails**. You can still skip the email for one change from the visit form.

## Your own templates

Templates you add under **Your own templates** show up in the send form for quotes and invoices:
a follow-up, a seasonal offer, a thank-you.

## Let automations follow up

**Automations**, in the same group, sends things for you when something happens, like thanking a
client when a job is done.

![Automations: press Turn on beside a ready-made one, then change its wording if you like](automations)

1. Under **Ready-made automations**, press **Turn on** beside one.
2. Change its wording or timing if you like.

**Recent activity** shows what your automations did, and what they skipped and why. Automations
come with some plans.
