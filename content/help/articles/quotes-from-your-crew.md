The person who looked at the job often knows best what it needs. Technicians can draft the quote
from a request they followed up, and the office checks it before it goes to the client.

## How it works

1. A technician marks their follow-up done and presses **Create quote**. A draft opens with the
   request's client and services.
2. They add what they found and the prices, then press **Ready for review**.
3. The office gets a notification under the bell ("A quote is ready to check"), and finds it in
   **Quotes**. The draft says who marked it ready, and when.
4. Someone in the office checks it, changes anything that needs it, and presses **Send quote**.

![A technician's draft, ready for review: check it, change anything needed, then press Send quote](quote-ready-for-review)

Technicians see only the quotes they drafted, under **Your quotes** on **My day**, until each one is
sent.

## Let some quotes go without a check

For simple, fixed-price work you can let technicians send quotes themselves. In
**Services & prices**, open a service and tick **Technicians can send without review**. A
technician's quote made only of those services, at their prices, can be sent by them straight
away. Anything else still comes to the office for review.

**Quotes sent without review**, on **Services & prices**, sets a cap: a quote over that total,
tax included, still comes to the office. Leave it blank for no cap.

## Who can do what

What a person can do depends on their role, set in **Team**. Technicians can quote the requests
assigned to them; sending their own quotes is a separate permission that's off for technicians.
