**Requests** is your inbox for new work. Requests sent through your online request form land
here on their own, and so do the calls, emails and walk-ins you log. Nothing on this page is
seen by your clients.

![The Requests page: the request form link, Log a request, and each request with its client, status and who's following it up](requests-list)

## Log a call, email or visit

1. Open **Requests** and press **Log a request**.
2. Choose the client. Search for someone you already have, or press **New client** and type
   their name and an email or phone number.
3. Under **How did they reach you?**, pick **Phone**, **Email**, **In person** or **Other**.
4. Tick the **Services** they asked about, if any, and say **What they need** in your own words.
5. If they mentioned timing, add it under **When**, like "Next week, mornings".
6. Press **Log request**.

To hand the request to someone for a site visit or a call, fill in **Follow-up** before you
press **Log request**. They'll see it on their day.

## Share your request form

If your request form is on, **Copy form link** at the top of **Requests** copies its address.
Put it on your website, in your email signature or in a text. Turn the form on or off in
**Settings** under **Request form**.

## Work through a request

Open a request to see what they need, their photos and the client's details.

![A request: the client, what they need, Internal notes, and Create quote at the top](request-page)

- **Mark in progress** shows your team you're on it. **Mark new** puts it back in the pile.
- **Internal notes**: type in **Add a note** and press **Add note**. Only your team sees notes.
- **Archive** clears it from the list when there's no work in it. You can still find it under the
  **Archived** tab and bring it back.

## Turn it into a quote

Press **Create quote**. A draft quote opens with the client, their property and the services they
asked for already filled in, at today's prices. The request moves to **Quoted**. Nothing reaches
your client until you send the quote.

A request has one draft quote at a time. If someone already started one, **Create quote** opens
that draft instead, so two people don't quote the same job. Once it's been sent, you can create
another.
