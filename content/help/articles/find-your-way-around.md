Shadetree takes a job from the first call to getting paid: a request, a quote your client
approves, visits on the schedule, an invoice, and the payment.

## Get set up

A new business starts on **Home** with **Get set up**: the steps that take you from nothing to
being paid.

![Home for a new business: work through the Get set up steps, from adding services to sending your first quote](home-get-set-up)

1. **Add your services and prices**: see [Add your services and prices](add-services-and-prices).
2. **Add a client**: a name and a phone number or email is enough. See
   [Add a client and keep their details](add-and-find-clients).
3. **Send your first quote**: see [Build a quote](build-a-quote) and
   [Send a quote, and what your client sees](send-a-quote).
4. **Get paid online**, where card payments are available: see
   [Take card payments online](take-card-payments).

Each step opens the real screen and brings you back. Want to see what clients get first? Press
**Preview a sample quote**.

## Take the tour

New here? **Take a 2-minute tour** points at the real screens, one at a time. You can stop
whenever you like, and take it again from the **Help** menu with **Take the tour again**.

The first time you open each main page, a short tip says what it's for. **Got it** hides it;
**Show tips again** in the **Help** menu brings them back.

## Where things are

The menu on the left (behind the menu button on a phone) has:

- **Home**: what needs you today, your visits and how the business is doing.
- **Requests**, **Clients**, **Quotes**, **Jobs**, **Invoices** and **Schedule**: the work, in
  the order it happens.
- **Team**, **Services & prices** and **Settings**: how your business runs.

The crew sees **My day** instead: only their own visits and follow-ups. What each person sees
depends on their role.

At the top of every page:

- **Help** (the question-mark button) searches these articles, opens **Help articles**, and has
  **Ask Shadetree for help**. See [Get help from the Shadetree team](get-help-from-shadetree).
- The bell shows your **Notifications**: new requests, approved quotes, payments and more.

Many pages have a **Learn more** link to the article about them.
