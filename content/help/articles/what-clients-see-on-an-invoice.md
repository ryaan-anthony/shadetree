When you send an invoice, your client gets a link to its own page. They don't need an account or
an app. To see it as they will, press **View as client** on the invoice.

![The invoice as your client sees it: they check the items and the balance due, then pay or download the PDF](client-invoice-page)

## What's on it

- Your logo, business name and contact details, in your brand color.
- **Invoice** and its number, who it's **Billed to**, the **Property**, when it was **Issued** and
  when it's **Due**.
- The items, discounts, tax and total, and any **Terms**.
- **Balance due**: what's left to pay after payments so far.
- **Download PDF** for their records.

## Paying online

If you take card payments, your client sees a **Pay** button with the balance, like "Pay $257.40".
If you let clients pay part of an invoice, they choose an **Amount to pay** and press **Pay now**. They pay on the card provider's secure
page, come back to **Thank you for your payment**, and you're told. See
[Take card payments online](take-card-payments).

Without card payments, they pay you as usual, and you record it: see
[Invoice finished work and record a payment](invoice-finished-work).

## Later

- Past its due date, the page says **Overdue** and the date it was due.
- Once it's paid, it says **Paid in full. Thank you!**
- If you void it, it says the invoice was cancelled, so nothing is due, and how to contact you.
- If you revise it, the page says you're updating the invoice, and they'll get the new version at
  the same link.
