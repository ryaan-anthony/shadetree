Cancelling one visit leaves the job and its other visits as they are. The visit stays in the job's
history.

![A job's visits: open a visit's ··· menu to reschedule, complete or cancel it](job-visits)

## Cancel a visit

1. Open the job. Under **Visits**, open the visit's **···** menu and choose **Cancel visit**.
2. Add a **Reason** if you like, such as "Rain".
3. If the client is emailed about visits, a box such as "Email Grace Chen about this" is ticked:
   they're told it's cancelled. Untick it to cancel without telling them.
4. Press **Cancel visit**. (**Keep visit** leaves it as it was.)

The visit comes off the schedule and off the crew's **My day**.

## Bring it back

While the job is open, choose **Restore** in the cancelled visit's **···** menu.

## Move it instead

Choose **Reschedule or reassign** in the visit's **···** menu, or drag it on the schedule. See
[Book visits on the schedule](book-visits-on-the-schedule).
