When your quote is ready, press **Send quote** at the top of it. You choose how it gets to your
client: by email from Shadetree, or by a link you send yourself.

![Email it, on the send page: To, CC, Subject and Message, and Send quote](send-quote-page)

## Email it

1. Check the **To** address. It's your client's email, if you have it.
2. Add anyone else under **CC**, separated by commas.
3. Change the **Subject** and **Message** if you like.
4. Press **Send quote**.

Your client gets your message, a button to open the quote and the quote as a PDF. If they reply,
the reply comes to you.

## Send the link yourself

Prefer to text or message your client? Under **Send the link yourself**, press **Get the link**,
then **Copy link**, and paste it into your own message. Getting the link marks the quote as sent.

## What your client sees

The link opens your quote with your name, logo and colors. Your client doesn't need an account
or an app.

![The quote as the client sees it: the work, the total and the buttons to answer](client-quote-page)

They can:

- **Approve quote**: they type their full name to sign, and you're told straight away. The record
  of exactly what they agreed to is kept with the quote.
- **Ask for changes**: they tell you what to change, and you're told.
- **Decline quote**: they can say why.

You're also told when they first open it. **Sent so far** on the send page lists every time you
sent it, and how.

## Sent the wrong price?

Open the quote and press **Revise**. The quote opens as a new revision you can edit. Until you
send it, your client sees that the quote is being updated, so they won't approve the old price.
When it's right, press **Send quote** again. Earlier versions are kept, and **See what was sent**
shows the last one.

Your plan's quote count goes up when a quote is first sent. Sending a revision or a copy doesn't
count again.
