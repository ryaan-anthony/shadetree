**Services & prices** is the list you pick from when you build a quote or an invoice. Set each
service up once with its usual price; you can still change the price on any quote.

![Services & prices: press Add service to add one, or open a service to change its price](services-list)

## Add a service

1. Open **Services & prices** and press **Add service**.
2. Type its **Name**, like "Lawn mowing".
3. Add a **Description for clients**. It's shown under the service on quotes and invoices.
4. Enter the **Default price**, and choose what it's **Per**: each, hour, visit, sq ft, linear ft,
   or custom for your own unit. Leave the price blank to price it on each quote.
5. Choose a **Category**, or type a new one, to keep the list tidy.
6. Add a **Typical duration (minutes)** if you know it. It helps fill in visit lengths.
7. Press **Add service**.

![Add a service: fill in the name, description, default price and unit, then press Add service](add-a-service)

## Start from common services

New to Shadetree? **Add starter services** offers common services for your trade, ready for
your prices.

## Change a price

Open the service, change the **Default price** and press **Save service**. New quotes and
invoices use the new price. Quotes and invoices you've already made keep the price they had,
so nothing you've sent changes behind your client's back.

## Tax rates

Your tax rates live in **Settings**, under **Money**, **Taxes**. Press **Add a tax rate**, give it a
name your clients will recognize, like "State sales tax", and its **Rate**, then press **Add rate**. Rates marked default are
added to taxable items on new quotes and invoices. You can change the rates on any single quote or
invoice.
