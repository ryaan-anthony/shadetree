Your hours shade the schedule, so it's clear when you're closed, and the schedule warns you when a
visit is booked outside them.

![Hours: set when you open and close each day and press Save hours, or press Add closure for a day off](hours-page)

## Your weekly hours

1. Open **Settings**, then **Hours**.
2. Under **Weekly hours**, set when you open and close each day, or mark a day **Closed**.
   **Set Mon–Fri, 8 am–5 pm** fills in a typical week to start from.
3. Press **Save hours**.

## Holidays and days off

Add the days you know you're closed, like Thanksgiving or a week's vacation:

1. Under **Closures**, press **Add closure**.
2. Give it a reason your team will recognize, like "Thanksgiving", and its date. For more than one
   day, add the last day too.
3. Tick **Only part of the day** for something like closing at noon on Christmas Eve.
4. Tick **Every year** for a holiday on the same date each year, like Christmas Day. (Not for
   holidays that move, like Thanksgiving.)
5. Press **Save closure**.

Your team sees closures on the schedule. Booking a visit then is still possible: the schedule
asks you to check first.
