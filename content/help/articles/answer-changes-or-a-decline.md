Your client's answer comes to you as a notification, and the quote's status changes. Their
message is on the quote, under **Client response**.

![A quote with Changes requested: read the client's message under Client response, then press Revise](quote-changes-requested)

## They asked for changes

The quote shows **Changes requested**, with what they wrote.

1. Open the quote and press **Revise**. Until you send the new version, your client's link says
   the quote is being updated, and they can't answer the old one.
2. Make the changes. The banner at the top repeats what they asked for.
3. Press **Send quote**. They get the new version; the earlier one is kept, and
   **See what was sent** shows it.

## They declined

The quote shows **Declined**, with their reason if they gave one. Nothing more happens on its
own. You can:

- **Revise** it and send a new version, if a different price or scope might work;
- start again with **Duplicate**, in the **···** menu;
- or **Archive** it, in the **···** menu, to clear it from your lists.

## It expired

After its valid-until date your client can't approve it. They can press
**Ask for an updated quote**, and you're told. To give them more time, open the quote and press
**Change** beside **Valid until**, pick a new date and press **Save date**. Their link stays the
same.
