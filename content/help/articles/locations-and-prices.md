If you work from more than one base, each location can have its own address, contact details,
time zone, hours, team and prices. Clients see the details of the location their work is with.
Locations come with plans that include more than one location.

![Locations: each location with its address and team; press Add location for another](locations-list)

## Add a location

1. Open **Settings**, then **Details**. Under **Address**, press **Add a location**. (Once you
   have more than one, they're under **Settings**, **Locations**, with **Add location**.)
2. Give it a **Name** clients will recognize, like "North side". They see it beside your
   business name.
3. Add its **Address** and **Contact details**. Replies to its emails go to its email, or your
   business email if it has none.
4. Check its **Time zone**: its visits, hours and reminders use it.
5. Choose its **Default taxes**: the business's defaults, or its own.
6. Under **Team**, tick who works there. Someone who works at only one location starts their new
   quotes and invoices there.
7. Press **Add location**.

Its hours and closures are set from the location's ··· menu, **Hours and closures**.

## Where each person's work starts

Under **Default locations**, on **Locations**, choose where new quotes, jobs and invoices from each
person start. **Automatic** uses the one location they work at, or your first location if they
work at several. They can still pick another location on any document.

## Different prices at each location

1. Open **Services & prices** and open a service.
2. Under **Prices by location**, enter the price at each location where it's different. Leave a
   location empty to use the default price there.
3. Press **Save service**.

![Prices by location: enter a price for a location, or leave it empty to use the default](location-prices)

New quotes and invoices use the price for their location. Quotes and invoices already made keep
their prices. If you move a quote to another location, Shadetree offers to change its prices to
that location's.

## Choosing the location on a quote, job or request

Quotes, invoices, jobs and requests have a **Location** field when you have more than one. Lists
can be filtered by location with the menu at the top (**All locations**).
