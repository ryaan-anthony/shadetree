Some work needs a look before you can quote it. Give the request to someone for a site visit or
a call: it goes on their day and on the schedule, and they report back with what they found.

## Assign the follow-up

1. Open the request. Under **Follow-up**, press **Assign**.
2. Under **Who follows it up**, choose the person.
3. Choose **Site visit** or **Call**.
4. Pick the **Day**, and a **Time** if it matters.
5. Change the **Estimated hours** if you like. Left empty, a site visit takes an hour and a call
   15 minutes.
6. Add **Instructions for them**: what to look at or ask. Only your team sees these.
7. Press **Assign**.

![Assign a follow-up: who follows it up, site visit or call, day, time, estimated hours and instructions](assign-follow-up)

The follow-up shows on the schedule and on their **My day**. To change it later, open the request
and press **Move or reassign**, or **Unassign**.

## What they see and do

On their phone, the follow-up is on **My day**. They open it to see what the client asked for,
the address with **Directions**, and **Call** and **Text** buttons.

![A follow-up on My day: what they asked for, getting there, site photos and notes, with Mark site visit done](my-day-request)

When they've looked, they:

1. Add **Site photos** and a note about what they saw, for whoever quotes it.
2. Press **Mark site visit done** (or **Mark call done**), and confirm with **Mark done**.

Then the office quotes it. If their role lets them, they can press **Create quote** and draft the
quote themselves: see [Quotes from your crew](quotes-from-your-crew). Or they press
**Pass back to** (followed by the name of whoever gave it to them) and, in **Pass it back**, say
what they found, so someone else can take it on.
