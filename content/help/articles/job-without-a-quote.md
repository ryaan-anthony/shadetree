When **Make a job when a quote is approved** is on (in **Settings**, **Quotes, jobs & invoices**),
jobs from approved quotes are made for you. For work agreed by phone or in person, make the job
yourself.

![New job: choose the client, give it a title, add the work to do, then press Create job](new-job)

## Make a job

1. Open **Jobs** and press **New job**.
2. Choose the **Client**, and the **Property** where the work is.
3. Give it a **Title**, like "Storm cleanup".
4. Add **Instructions for the crew**: everyone on its visits sees these. **Internal notes** are for
   Owners and Admins only.
5. Under **Work to do**, add what you'll do: pick from your services, or press
   **Add a custom item**. It's optional, but the invoice uses these items.
6. Press **Create job**.

Then book it: press **Schedule visit** on the job. See
[Book visits on the schedule](book-visits-on-the-schedule).

## Change what a job covers

While a job is open, press **Edit items** under **Work to do** on the job. Add, change or remove
items, and press **Save job**. Its invoice, when you create it, copies the items as they are then.
