Your clients see your business details on every quote, invoice and email, so start here.

![Settings, Details: fill in your business profile, contact details, address and time zone, then press Save changes](business-details)

## Your details

1. Open **Settings**. **Details** opens first.
2. Under **Business profile**, check your **Business name** and choose your **Industry**.
3. Under **Contact details**, add your **Business email**, **Phone** and **Website**. Clients use
   these to reach you.
4. Under **Address**, add where clients and documents can reach you.
5. Check your **Time zone** and **Currency**. Visit times and due dates use your time zone.
6. Press **Save changes**.

## What else is in Settings

Settings is grouped at the top of the page:

- **Business**: **Details**, **Hours** (when you're open, and days you're closed), **Locations**
  (once you have more than one), **Branding**
  (your logo and color on what clients see) and **Request form** (your online form for new work).
- **Money**: **Quotes, jobs & invoices** (how long quotes stay valid, numbering, terms and days
  to pay), **Taxes** and **Card payments** (getting paid online).
- **Messages**: **Emails to clients** (the wording of each email) and **Automations**.
- **Plan and data**: your **Plan**, and **Your data** to download it.

Changes to what new quotes and invoices start with don't change documents you've already made.

## Who can change Settings

The owner, Admins, and anyone whose role lets them change the business's settings. Others don't
see Settings in their menu.
