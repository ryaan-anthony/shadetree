Your logo and brand color are on everything your clients see: quotes, invoices, emails and their
client pages. Your own screens stay Shadetree green.

![Branding: drop your logo, choose a brand color, check the preview, then press Save branding](branding)

## Your logo

1. Open **Settings**, then **Branding**.
2. Under **Logo**, press **browse** and choose the file, or drag it onto **Drop your logo here**.
   Use a PNG, JPEG or WebP file. A wide logo on a transparent background works best.
3. Press **Save branding**.

Without a logo, clients see your business name and initials. To change it later, use
**Replace logo**; to take it off, **Remove logo**.

## Your brand color

Under **Brand color**, pick one of the **Presets**, or choose **Custom** and type its **Hex** code
(like #9A3412). The **Preview** shows a quote as clients will see it, with the color on its
button. Press **Save branding**.

## Check an email

**Send me a test email** sends you a sample of a client email with your logo and color, so you
can see it in your own inbox.
