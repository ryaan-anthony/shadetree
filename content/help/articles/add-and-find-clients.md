A client is a person or a business you work for. Their page gathers their properties, requests,
quotes, jobs, invoices and what they owe. Clients never sign in to Shadetree: they get links to
their quotes and invoices.

## Add a client

1. Open **Clients** and press **Add client**.
2. Type their **Name**: a person, like "Maria Lopez", or a business. Add a
   **Company** if there is one.
3. Under **How to reach them**, add an email or a phone number. **Add email** and **Add phone**
   add more.
4. Under **Properties**, add the **Street address** where the work happens. **Add property**
   adds another.
5. Add **Tags** if you group your clients, like "commercial, weekly", and any **Notes** for your
   team.
6. Tick **Tax exempt** if no tax should be added to their quotes and invoices.
7. Press **Add client**.

![Add client: type their name, how to reach them and their street address, then press Add client](add-client-form)

If the email or phone matches someone you already have, Shadetree shows them before you save,
so you don't end up with the same client twice.

## Find a client

Type part of a name, phone number, email or address in the search box on **Clients**. Results
appear as you type.

## Everything about a client

Open a client to see their contact details and properties, their **Work** (requests, quotes,
jobs and invoices), their **Balance** (what's owed on invoices that are out) and a timeline of
what's happened.

![A client's page: press New quote to quote them, or Edit to change their details](client-page)

- **New quote** starts a quote for them.
- **Edit** changes their details.
- The **···** menu's **Archive** hides a client you no longer work with. Their history stays, and
  **Restore** brings them back.
- The **···** menu's **Erase their details…** removes their personal details for good, when they ask
  you to.
